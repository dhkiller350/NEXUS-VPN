FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NEXUS VPN?

NEXUS VPN is a privacy-focused virtual private network service that encrypts your internet connection using the WireGuard protocol. It routes your traffic through secure servers worldwide, hiding your IP address and protecting your data from surveillance.

Why WireGuard?

WireGuard is a modern VPN protocol that's significantly faster and more secure than legacy protocols like OpenVPN and IPSec. It has a smaller codebase (making it easier to audit), uses state-of-the-art cryptography, and delivers better performance on both mobile and desktop devices.

Do you keep any logs?

No. NEXUS VPN operates under a strict no-logs policy. We do not track, monitor, or store any of your browsing activity, connection timestamps, or IP addresses. Your privacy is our top priority.

Can I use NEXUS VPN on multiple devices?

Yes! Depending on your plan, you can connect 1 to unlimited devices simultaneously. You can also download WireGuard configuration files to use with native WireGuard clients on any platform.

What is a Kill Switch?

A kill switch is a safety feature that automatically blocks all internet traffic if your VPN connection drops unexpectedly. This prevents your real IP address and unencrypted data from being exposed, even for a split second.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! Our Free plan lets you try NEXUS VPN with 3 server locations at no cost. You can upgrade to Pro or Enterprise at any time to unlock all features and server locations.
